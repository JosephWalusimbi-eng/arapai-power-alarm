Arapai Power Alarm
Privacy Policy

Effective 17 October 2026

Arapai Power Alarm does not collect, store or share any personal information. Everything the app does happens on your own computer.

This policy covers the Arapai Power Alarm app for Windows, published by Arapai Technologies International ("we", "us").

Information we collect

We collect none. Arapai Power Alarm:

  • does not connect to the internet;
  • does not ask for your name, email address or any account;
  • does not include analytics, tracking or advertising;
  • does not send any information to us or to anyone else.

What the app uses on your device

  • Power status. The app reads whether your laptop is plugged in and the battery percentage, from Windows, twice a second. It uses this only to decide when to show an alarm. It is never saved or sent anywhere.
  • Your settings. The app saves your choices (which warnings are on, your battery levels and your alarm sound) in a small settings file on your computer. If you choose your own sound file, the settings file records where that file is on your computer.
  • Startup. The app starts when you sign in to Windows. You can turn this off at any time in Windows Settings → Apps → Startup.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling Arapai Power Alarm removes the app and its settings file from your computer. There is nothing stored anywhere else to delete.

Sharing with others

We do not sell, rent or share any information, because we do not collect any.

Microsoft handles app downloads, updates and any reviews you write through the Microsoft Store. That is covered by the Microsoft Privacy Statement, not by this policy.

Children

The app collects no information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes how it handles information, we will update this policy and its effective date before that version is released.
